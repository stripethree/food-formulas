# Rustic Shrimp Toasts

### Components

* 1 pound large shrimp, peeled, deveined, rinsed, patted dry, halved lengthwise
* 4 scallions, cut into 1-inch pieces
* 3 tablespoons drained capers
* 1 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
* 1/2 teaspoon Diamond Crystal or 1/4 teaspoon Morton kosher salt
* 1/2 cup plus 2 tablespoons store-bought or homemade aioli
* 4 half-inch-thick slices country-style bread
* 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
* 1/3 cup coarsely chopped fresh parsley leaves
* 2 lemons, cut into wedges

### Assembly

1. Mix shrimp, scallions, capers, red pepper flakes, salt, and 1/2 cup aioli in a medium bowl to combine.
2. Spread bread slices with the remaining 2 tablespoons aioli. Arrange shrimp mixture, cut side down, in a snug, even layer on the bread all the way to the edges. Pack capers and scallions on, under, and between shrimp wherever they will fit. Press shrimp mixture firmly into the bread to ensure good contact.
3. Place assembled toasts on a plate and chill for at least 10 minutes and up to 30 minutes (this helps the shrimp adhere to the bread).
4. Heat broiler to low. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large ovenproof skillet over high heat. Cook 2 toasts, bread side down, for 15 seconds, then transfer skillet to oven. Broil, watching carefully, until toasts are golden and crisp in spots and shrimp turn pink and curl slightly, 5-7 minutes.
5. Transfer toasts to a cutting board. Repeat with remaining 1 tablespoon oil and 2 toasts.
6. Cut each toast in half and transfer to a platter. Top with parsley and serve with lemon wedges.

### Notes

* Sourced from [this recipe](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/rustic-shrimp-toasts).
* Recipe makes 8 pieces (4 toasts, each cut in half).
* Don't skimp on the lemon!
