# Cappuccino Muffins with Espresso Spread

### Components

**Espresso Spread**

* 4 ounces cream cheese, cubed and softened
* 1 tablespoon sugar
* 1/2 teaspoon instant coffee granules
* 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
* 1/4 cup miniature semisweet chocolate chips

**Muffins**

* 2 cups all-purpose flour
* 3/4 cup sugar
* 2 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
* 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* 1 cup milk
* 2 tablespoons instant coffee granules
* 1/2 cup butter or margarine, melted
* 1 egg, beaten
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
* 3/4 cup miniature semisweet chocolate chips

### Assembly

1. Make the espresso spread: Combine cream cheese, sugar, coffee granules, and vanilla in a food processor and blend until smooth. Stir in chocolate chips. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
2. Preheat oven to 375°F. Grease or line a muffin tin.
3. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt.
4. In a separate bowl, dissolve the coffee granules in the milk. Stir in the melted butter, beaten egg, and vanilla.
5. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Fold in the chocolate chips — do not overmix.
6. Fill muffin cups about two-thirds full. Bake for 17-20 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
7. Serve warm with the chilled espresso spread.

### Notes

* Recipe makes about 14 muffins.
