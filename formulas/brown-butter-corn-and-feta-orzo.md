# Brown Butter Corn and Feta Orzo with Crispy Prosciutto

### Components

* 1 pound dry orzo pasta
* 3 ounces prosciutto, torn
* 4 tablespoons salted butter
* 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
* 1 shallot, finely chopped
* 1 jalapeño, seeded if desired, and chopped
* 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
* 1/2 cup fresh basil, roughly chopped
* 1/4 cup fresh chives, chopped
* 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
* 2 tablespoons champagne or apple cider vinegar
* 2 teaspoons honey
* 4 ears grilled or steamed corn, kernels removed from the cob
* 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
* 8 ounces crumbled feta cheese
* 2 avocados, sliced
* salt and pepper, to taste

### Assembly

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the orzo to al dente according to package directions. Drain and return pasta to the pot.
2. Cook the prosciutto in a large skillet over medium heat until crispy, about 2 minutes per side. Remove from the skillet and set aside.
3. Add the butter to the same skillet. Allow the butter to brown until it smells toasted and is a deep golden color, about 3-4 minutes. Stir in the olive oil, shallot, jalapeño, and thyme. Cook 1-2 minutes, then remove from heat.
4. Pour the browned butter mixture over the hot orzo and toss to combine.
5. Add the basil, chives, lemon juice, vinegar, and honey. Season with salt and pepper and toss. Stir in the corn, tomatoes, and feta.
6. Top with the crispy prosciutto and avocado. Serve warm or at room temperature.

### Notes

* Sourced from [this recipe](https://www.halfbakedharvest.com/brown-butter-corn-and-feta-orzo/).
* Recipe makes about 6 servings.
* Ready in about 25 minutes.
