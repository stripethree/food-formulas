# Lime Shrimp Dragon Noodles

### Components

**Sauce**

* 2 tablespoons sriracha
* 2 tablespoons soy sauce
* 2 tablespoons brown sugar
* 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice

**Noodles and Shrimp**

* 2 packages ramen noodles, flavor packets discarded
* 2 tablespoons butter
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1/2 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined
* 1 lime, cut into wedges for serving

**Garnish**

* 2 green onions, thinly sliced
* Fresh cilantro leaves

### Assembly

1. Place the shrimp in a colander and rinse under cool water until thawed. Drain well and pat dry.
2. In a small bowl, stir together the sriracha, soy sauce, brown sugar, and lime juice. Set aside.
3. Bring a pot of water to a boil. Add the ramen noodles and cook according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
4. While the noodles cook, melt the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the garlic and sauté for about 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the shrimp and cook until pink and opaque, about 3-5 minutes.
5. Add the drained noodles to the skillet with the shrimp. Pour the sauce over top and toss everything together until well coated.
6. Serve topped with sliced green onions and cilantro, with lime wedges on the side.
