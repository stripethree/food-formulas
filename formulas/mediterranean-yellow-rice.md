# Mediterranean Yellow Rice

### Components

* 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
* 1 medium onion, finely chopped
* 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
* 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
* 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
* 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
* 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
* 1 1/2 cups long-grain white rice
* 2 1/4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
* 1/4 cup toasted pine nuts
* Fresh parsley and cilantro, for garnish

### Assembly

1. Heat olive oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic and sauté until translucent, about 3-4 minutes.
2. Stir in the turmeric, cumin, coriander, and salt. Add the rice and stir to coat with the aromatic mixture.
3. Pour in the broth and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes.
4. Remove from heat and let sit, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork, stir in the pine nuts, and garnish with fresh parsley and cilantro.

### Notes

* Sourced from [this recipe](https://www.veggiessavetheday.com/mediterranean-yellow-rice/).
* Recipe makes about 4 servings.
