# Honey-Sriracha Popcorn

### Components

* 1/2 cup popcorn kernels (yields about 16 cups popped)
* 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
* 1/4 cup honey
* 2 tablespoons sriracha (add more for extra heat)
* 1 tablespoon brown sugar
* 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
* 1/4 teaspoon baking soda

### Assembly

1. Preheat oven to 250°F. Line two large rimmed baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. Pop the popcorn kernels and transfer to a very large heatproof bowl, removing any unpopped kernels.
3. Melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Add the honey and brown sugar and stir to combine. Bring to a boil and cook, stirring constantly, for about 3-4 minutes.
4. Remove from heat and stir in the sriracha, salt, and baking soda (the mixture will foam slightly).
5. Pour the hot syrup over the popcorn immediately, stirring and folding to coat evenly.
6. Spread the coated popcorn in an even layer across the prepared baking sheets. Bake for 45-60 minutes, stirring every 15 minutes, until the coating is set and the popcorn is crisp.
7. Let cool completely on the baking sheets before serving or storing.

### Notes

* Sourced from [this recipe](https://www.epicurious.com/recipes/food/views/high-honey-sriracha-popcorn).
* The baking step is key for getting a crispy, set coating — don't skip it.
* The original recipe includes scallions or fresh herbs as garnish; omit if preferred.
