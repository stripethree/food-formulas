# Lemon Herb Mediterranean Chicken

### Components

**Marinade / Dressing**

* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice (about 1 lemon)
* 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
* 2 tablespoons water
* 2 teaspoons garlic, minced
* 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
* 2 teaspoons dried basil
* 1 teaspoon dried oregano
* 1 teaspoon salt

**Chicken**

* 1 pound skinless boneless chicken thigh fillets (or chicken breasts)

### Assembly

1. In a bowl or jar, whisk together all marinade ingredients. Pour half over the chicken and marinate for at least 15-30 minutes (or up to 2 hours in the refrigerator). Reserve the remaining marinade to use as a dressing.
2. Heat a grill pan over medium heat. Grill the chicken for 5-7 minutes per side until cooked through and slightly charred.
3. Let rest for 5 minutes, then slice. Drizzle with the reserved marinade/dressing before serving.

### Notes

* Sourced from [this recipe](https://cafedelites.com/grilled-lemon-herb-mediterranean-chicken-salad-recipe/).
* Recipe makes about 4 servings.
* The original recipe serves this over a salad with Romaine lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, avocado, and Kalamata olives — also great served over Mediterranean yellow rice.
